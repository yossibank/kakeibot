Kakeibot is a simple household budget book that is easy to keep up and fun to look back on. No ads.

The app is in Japanese and amounts are in yen.

■ Log quickly
・Save frequent expenses as templates and log them in one or two taps
・Built-in calculator handles tax on the spot
・Record transfers between accounts and fees as well as expenses and income
・Fixed costs such as rent and subscriptions are logged automatically every month or year
・Scan a receipt to read the items, total, date and store

■ Know what you can still spend
・Set budgets for the whole month and for each category
・A half-circle meter on the home screen shows what is left this month
・Start the month on payday and the week on Monday
・Carry unused budget over to the next period

■ Clear reviews
・See totals of spending and income by week, month and year
・Breakdowns by category and account, compared with the previous period
・Track loan repayments and investment plans

■ Make it yours
・Pick an app color from the themes
・Choose text size, line spacing, corner roundness and background patterns
・Create category icons and calendar stamps from symbols, emoji or short text

■ Help to keep going
・Reward savings: reach your own goals and fill a virtual piggy bank
・Reminders: get notified only on days you have not logged by your chosen time
・Widgets: check what is left and log right from the Home Screen and Lock Screen

■ About your data
・Your data stays on your device and in your own iCloud. It is never sent to outside servers
・Export and import to keep a copy of your data

■ Pro (one-time purchase)
Buy once and use it forever. Family Sharing members can use it too.
・Sync records and settings across your iPhones with iCloud
・Share accounts with family and log together. Invited people do not need Pro
・Create your own theme by choosing colors
・Use rounded, handwritten, dot and pop typefaces
